Jane Doe

Role: new user with dog

User Story: As a new user, I want to easily create a new account that gives me recommendations for coffee shops that suit my specific lifestyle.

Technology: iPhone, laptop

Jane Doe is a busy dog trainer who is extremely passionate about her vegan lifestyle and dog. Coffee shops do not always have vegan options, and if they do, its limited. Jane wants to have personalized recommendations to make her coffee shop search easier.

She typically uses her phone to search for coffee shops while on campus and often visits locations she has never been to before.

Behaviors and Attitudes:

  • Studies in coffee shops 3-5 times per week
  • Relies heavily on WiFi
  • Prefers quiet environments with minimal distractions
  • Values comfort and available seating
  • Often studies for several hours at a time
  • Enjoys trying new coffee shops but dislikes wasting time on places that do not meet her needs.

Scenario

Emma has a large exam coming up and needs a quiet coffee shop with reliable WiFi and available outlets so she can spend the afternoon studying.

She opens the App to find a location that matches her needs.

Frustrations

  • Online reviews are not always reliable
  • I want to discover vegan cafes and get reccomondations
  • I want to find quiet cafes to keep my dog comforable

Goals

  • Find shops for my specific needs
  • Avoid noisy or crowded locations
  • Have an easy experience creating an account

Interaction Flow

Goal: As a new user, I want to create an account and have this program create a personalized experience for dog friendly, comfy, quiet cafes.

  • Jane Doe signs into the coffee website for the first time
  • Jane selects create an account
  • Prompts ask Jane to input a username, password, and preferences
  • Jane selects "dog friendly" "cozy" "quiet"
  • Website logs her in and takes Jane to the homepage
  • Homepage displays recommendations and a navigation bar
  • App displays ranked matches that include: distance, WiFi rating, noise rating
  • She selects the highest ranked match
  • App displays her chosen coffee shop's profile page
  • She saves this location to her favorites
  • After her study session, she reopens the app
  • App prompts her to rate her experience
  • System grabs data from API and other users similar to Jane's selected preferences as a baseline to give recommendations
  • Jane clicks "Sign out" on navigation bar
